## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The network_topology construct models a topology-discovery exporter, not snmp_exporter or Alloy's prometheus.exporter.snmp. AI Factory designs still poll SNMP for rack PDU metering, cooling distribution units and some switch health, and SNMP is the only practical path for most facility-side equipment. Metric names come from snmp_exporter generator output for real MIBs, so the construct must follow a pinned generated snmp.yml.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 The construct emits snmp_exporter metric shapes for if_mib plus at least one PDU module and one cooling module drawn from real published MIBs, with modules selectable per device declaration
- [ ] #2 Per-target scrape meta-series (up, scrape duration, walk duration and the snmp_exporter collection-health series) are emitted, with failure modes for device unreachable, SNMPv3 authentication failure and slow walks
- [ ] #3 A device declared once can feed both this construct and network_topology without an identity collision, and the interplay is documented
- [ ] #4 Vendor MIB objects with no published generator module are PENDING in cantfind.md

- [ ] #6 Rates, counts and cardinality in any shipped blueprint are plausible for a real deployment, with the real-world basis stated in a comment; nothing is raised to force emission
