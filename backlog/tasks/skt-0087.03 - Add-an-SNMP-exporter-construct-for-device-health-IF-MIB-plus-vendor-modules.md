## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The network_topology construct models a topology-discovery exporter, not snmp_exporter or Alloy's prometheus.exporter.snmp. AI Factory designs still poll SNMP for rack PDU metering, cooling distribution units and some switch health, and SNMP is the only practical path for most facility-side equipment. Metric names come from snmp_exporter generator output for real MIBs, so the construct must follow a pinned generated snmp.yml.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
