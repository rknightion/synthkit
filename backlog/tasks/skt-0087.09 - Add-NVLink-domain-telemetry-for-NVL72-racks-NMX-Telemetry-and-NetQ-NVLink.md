## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
An NVL72 rack is one NVLink domain of 72 GPUs joined through NVSwitch trays. NMX-Telemetry collects from the domain over gNMI and pushes OTLP from its built-in exporter, NMX-Controller manages partitions, and NetQ NVLink provides the domain and partition view and can re-export OTLP. The degraded-domain journey needs domain state, partition state, NVSwitch health, per-port link state and errors, and recovery activity, alongside NMX logs. Per-port telemetry is one of the largest cardinality drivers in these estates, and the port count itself has to be confirmed rather than assumed.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
