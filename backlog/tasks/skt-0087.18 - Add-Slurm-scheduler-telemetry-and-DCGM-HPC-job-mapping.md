## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Many AI Factories schedule on Slurm (BCM-managed, or Slurm on Kubernetes) instead of or alongside Run:ai. There the job-to-GPU join comes from dcgm-exporter's HPC job mapping, written by Slurm prolog and epilog scripts, which replaces a label per job and has its own churn profile. Lower priority than the Kubernetes scheduler path, but needed to cover the generic AI Factory case.
