## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
AI Factory racks draw tens to over a hundred kilowatts. Air-cooled PCIe GPU racks are metered at the PDU over SNMP. Liquid-cooled NVL72 racks report rack power from their power shelves over the out-of-band network and depend on a coolant distribution unit, read over SNMP or Modbus, for secondary-loop supply and return temperature, flow, pressure, pump state and leak detection. No CDU vendor ships a Prometheus exporter, so collection is snmp_exporter or a Modbus exporter, and a cooling fault shows up first as GPU thermal throttling. Facility-side BMS telemetry is a separate follow-up.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
