## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
A Rancher capture environment plus its capture, tagged `substrate: rancher`.

Rancher is the self-managed case: no cloud controller manager, node identity that comes from the provisioner rather than a cloud API, and a control plane that is fully scrapeable in a way no managed offering is. It is the closest substrate to the on-premises estates synthkit is asked to model, and nothing in the corpus represents it today.

Lower priority than AKS and GKE: the two managed clouds close the larger evidence gap first. Sequence it after them.
