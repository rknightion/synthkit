## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
A causal resource's demand is declared, not derived from upstream admission, so when an upstream failure stops calls the bound construct still shows the declared volume (loop58 L11: SQS sent 3600 while the publisher got 0; Lambda 3600 vs 2356 intake calls). Derive demand from the caller's admitted share (demand.from or app call flow) so app and construct counts agree under upstream failures.
