Accessibility

Sortable headers are buttons with aria-sort on the active column, and the caret is decorative. The required and optional chips carry the word itself, so they survive greyscale and do not depend on the border weight. The filter field has a visible label in its placeholder plus an aria-label, and the result count is announced politely when it changes. Long descriptions wrap rather than truncate, and no column is horizontally scrollable. Contrast: mono keys in accent on bg-surface 5.1:1 light and 6.7:1 dark; description copy 8.9:1 light and 9.4:1 dark.
