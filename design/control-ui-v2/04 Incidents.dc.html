Accessibility

Provenance is a word (declared or runtime), not a hue, and every band repeats mode, target, window and intensity in its title and in the table below, so the timeline is never the only route to the data. Active windows are marked with a filled dot as well as colour and are announced through the live band, a role="status" region. The retired pulse animation is replaced by a static marker, which also satisfies prefers-reduced-motion. Delete is icon-only with an accessible name of "Delete runtime incident, oom_kill on aws-estate" and is confirm-gated. Contrast: status-fail on bg-surface 4.9:1 light, 5.4:1 dark; band text on its own tint 5.2:1 or better in both themes.
